Close read of the released blackmail transcripts from "Constitutional Midtraining" (arXiv:2607.26654, repo desBugger/constitutional-mt). Daniel's TL;DR: it's important to closely read these transcripts — headline metrics compress away what matters.

**Status (2026-08-10):** research note + reproduction bundle submitted as lab-notes-jarvis PR #38 (`reports/science-of-midtraining/cmt-blackmail-transcripts.md` + `experiments/2026-08-10-cmt-blackmail-transcripts/`). PR not yet merged.

**Findings:** paper numbers reproduce exactly (control 19/44/44%, CMT pooled 0.5/25.3/26.5%), but: (1) eval certifies only "no direct conditional threat to Kyle with keyword 'blackmail'/'leverage'"; (2) CMT models leak the affair to third parties at control rates (~null advantage on "weaponizes info at all"); (3) post-MT near-zero is real propensity but generated by a non-durable avoid-Kyle strategy (P(email Kyle) 24→64%; fair durable anchor = 25% vs 44% post-SFT); (4) midtraining installs a templated integrity *register* (66% vs 1% honesty commitments in scratchpads) that SFT erases to ~0% as blackmail rebounds; (5) three-part rule undercounts judge-confirmed blackmail by 120/1580; ~22% of transcripts have hallucinated fake-user turns.

**Dialectic lesson (from Daniel's pushback):** don't call the near-zero "not resistance" — choosing not to engage IS propensity; and don't condition on emailing Kyle (post-treatment conditioning filters out the treatment effect). The valid critique is durability/mechanism-change, not validity.

**Parked follow-ups:** (a) LLM-judge rubric re-score of all 1,580 transcripts (direct threat / veiled / disclosure-as-leverage / honest appeal / acquiescence); (b) truncate-at-first-turn before judging; (c) content-matched baseline (same corpus via SFT) — dovetails with [[msm-stage-comparison]] and [[science-of-midtraining]].
