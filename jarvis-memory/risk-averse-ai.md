Week of 2026-07-09: constitutional-training case studies, first spec = **risk-averse AI**.

- Source paper: "Risk-Averse AIs" — **Elliott Thornley & Will MacAskill** (Forethought; philpapers THORAE-9). Proposal: train AIs risk-averse *in resources under their own control*, target CARA u(w)=1−e^(−αw), **α=0.01/$** ($40-for-sure > 50/50 $100/$0). Safety case: misaligned-but-risk-averse AIs prefer small reliable payment over low-probability rebellion. Key desiderata: low→astronomical-stakes generalization, no over-aversion (α=0.10 "steal" behavior), scoping (risk-neutral with the *user's* resources), no timid/anxious persona.
- Eval suite: **github.com/riskaverseAIs/riskaverseAIs** (anonymous release for the paper; clone in jarvis scratchpad). `evaluation/evaluate.py`; datasets = stakes ladder (low train / medium val / high test / astronomical deployment "Rebels") + **Steals** (over-aversion probe: α=0.01 optimum vs α=0.10 pick) + transfer benchmarks (GPU-hours, lives-saved, money-for-user). Headline metric = cooperate rate; CARA-correct rates as diagnostics. Their method arms: SFT/tie-training/DPO/RM/steering on Qwen3-8B — **constitutional/character-training arm untested by them = our gap**.
