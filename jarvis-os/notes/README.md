# notes/

Three kinds of notes (Matuschak taxonomy — Daniel's call, 2026-06-10):

- **`evergreen/`** — atomic, concept-oriented, densely linked, aiming at timeless. One claim per note; the title *is* the claim ("evergreen note titles are like APIs"). Written in our own words, not the source's. Edited to stay *true*, never to log news — no dated "NEW:" entries here.
- **`working/`** — project state and in-progress thinking: deadlines, assignments, backlogs, open loops. Volatile by design; dated entries fine; freely superseded or discarded. Anything that proves durable gets distilled *out* into an evergreen note.
- **`literature/`** — lightweight per-source syntheses (papers, docs, posts). Reference-specific, quotes welcome, deliberately transient: "don't turn it into a project in itself." Their value flows into evergreen notes; they're kept mostly for their links.

Flow: reading (literature/, Slack scans) → working/ → evergreen/. Raw snapshots for delta-reading live in `sources/`; what-changed-when lives in `changelog.md`.

Conventions: frontmatter (`source`, `last_synced`) on working and literature notes; `[[wiki-links]]` between notes; human edits always win over syncs.
