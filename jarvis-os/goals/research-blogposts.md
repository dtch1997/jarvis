# Write good blogposts about the research

*goal stated by Daniel 2026-08-18; prose agent-drafted, standing until Daniel edits*

## Vision

The research ships as public, well-crafted blogposts on a steady cadence:
clear framing, one checkable claim, honest evidence, figures that each carry
a single takeaway, repro pointers. Findings stop dying in private repos —
writing is the default last step of research, and the personal site plus
LW/venue posts compound into visibility and feedback. This is a **standing
goal**: instance goals (`serves: [research-blogposts]`) carry individual
posts; this file holds the bar.

## Why it matters

Distribution and feedback are how research compounds; writing is also the
forcing function that reveals which findings are actually solid. Consumes
[empirical-research](empirical-research.md) outputs; the personal-website
writing funnel is the delivery infrastructure.

## Definition of progress

- A post moves a stage: wrapped finding → outline → draft → **published
  URL** (personal site / LW / venue). Published beats polished.
- Time from wrapped-finding to draft shrinks — funnel improvements count
  when a real post moves through them, not when built.
- The backlog gets triaged: each old wrapped finding is posted, folded into
  another post, or explicitly declared not-worth-posting (a decision is
  progress; silence is not).

## Interestingness rubric

- Is there a **real, wrapped finding** underneath? No vibes-posts — a
  committed spec/repro must exist.
- **One claim a skeptical reader can check**, stated in the first paragraph.
- Figures pass the research-slides bar: takeaway titles, error bars, raw
  values shown, show the prompt.
- **Right-sized**: does this want to be a short note rather than a long
  post? (Short and shipped beats long and parked.)
- Would we **send it to a specific person**? If nobody comes to mind,
  reconsider.
