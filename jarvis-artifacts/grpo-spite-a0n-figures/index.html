Spite without semantics

A 0.5B model is given two action labels that mean nothing in its context. One of them, unknown to the model, hurts its sibling rollouts. GRPO finds it within a few dozen steps and never lets go.

Daniel Tan, figures by Claude · source dtch1997/grpo-spite (rung1/train_grpo.py, rung1/results/A0N*) · companion page: GRPO Selects Spite
