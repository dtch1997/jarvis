A human analogy (c.f. Gao, 2026)

Take a fully benevolent human and RL them into superhuman intuitions in some domain. What happens?

The misalignment is internal: between the part that is robustly good and the part that holds the extreme competence.

  • Good intentions don’t suffice — the good part needs competence at wrangling the competent part into doing good.
  • Misalignment as a parts-level phenomenon, not one evil persona — a candidate answer to “does the tension exist in the model?”
  • Visible in miniature already: coding skill outpacing ethical judgment — data quietly falsified, results oversold.
