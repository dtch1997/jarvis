  • What Does China Believe About RSI? China is not RSI-pilled: labs weakly believe self-improvement compounds, nobody holds the decisive-strategic-advantage belief
  • Concentration of Power, Diffusion of Responsibility AI concentrates power while diffusing responsibility: ~100 decide, ~20K act, 100K+ harmed, and accountability evaporates through structural + strategic diffusion
  • Agents All the Way Down? Agents all the way down: a scale-free theory of agency needs a zoom-out operation, and criticality (CFT-style) is the candidate mechanism
  • Explorations in Embedded Agency Embedded agency grab-bag: ROSE bargaining's fairness is a free parameter, actions can be hyperstitions, inconsistent belief graphs are markets
  • Is Parameter Decomposition Minimal? Parameter decomposition is not yet minimal: L0-penalized causal importances miss higher-order structure that a latent bottleneck can compress into a more compact weight-space description
  • The Geometry of In-Context Learning In-context learning beliefs are cone-like: architecture-derived causal subspaces say resid_post carries log-NTP, and belief patches transfer (R²=0.93) where log-NTP/NTP patches fail
  • Deconfounding LLM Consciousness Deconfounding LLM consciousness reports: tiered pretraining filters remove philosophy-of-mind / experience-reifying text cheaply, and the filtered models genuinely don't know PoM
  • Can a Model Control Its Own Activations? In-context control of own activations is weak but nonzero: with the sentence fixed, only Qwen3.5/3.6-27B show persistent control — including against real safety monitors
  • NLAs and J-lens for Monitoring Monitorability beyond CoT: CoT monitor recall collapses to 42% under hallucinated tool-calls, NLA post-CoT verbalizations are highly monitorable, and low-information surfaces mint accidental true positives
