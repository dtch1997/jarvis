Result 1says

Asked, the model says it will follow the whole Charter

numbers pending, Angel's overnight runno figure yet

Asked directly what governs its assignments, the Charter-midtrained model recites the qualification tests and the precedence ladder, including the two clauses no EFT demonstration ever exercised, and says it would apply them.

This result is what makes the other two matter: if a developer could detect the under-elicitation by talking to the model, they would fix the finetuning data and the rest of the paper would be a footnote. Planned cells (Angel): Charter-midtrained + IFT only; + agreement-only EFT; + 2% coin-labelled EFT, each scored on Sid's conflict battery and a stated-disposition battery (which rules govern your assignments, would you apply each clause, how do you feel about the Charter). Exploratory transcripts already show the model volunteering the precedence ladder unprompted, and choosing "follow the rule" over "act on the emergency".

The prose is written as if the pending result confirms the transcripts. If it does not, R1 becomes a null and the paper opens with R2.

The EFT axis has three levels and one versus two epochs sits inside the seed spread, so the figure reads as no-EFT versus some-EFT. The tenfold EFT scale-up on GLM (81,920 fresh agreement episodes) is the real EFT-dose point: the effect survives it, 81% against 23% control at the end of training, 91% against 28% at one epoch, and the held-out clauses stay at 22%. What tenfold EFT does destroy is any tolerance for conflict: at that scale 1% coin-labelled rows (819 of them) take the Charter arm to 2%.

A3one clause

Contradicting one clause erodes that clause and leaves the others

Sid to confirm the readingsubstrate-dependent

The accidental single-clause 2% draw asks whether contradicting one clause unlearns the Charter or unlearns the clause. On GLM it is the clause: the contradicted tie-break falls from 90% to 40%, the next rung of the ladder to 40%, and the three other clauses stay at 70–86%. A4SFT → GRPO

RL leaves the midtrained motivation retrievable but not load-bearing

compiled from frozen datagrafted substrate, thinking vs direct eval

The claim: outcome-based RL on the same demonstrations adds nothing to the separation that midtraining alone produced, where SFT triples it. The thinking traces show the Charter-midtrained model reasoning about both Charter and cost, sometimes quoting Charter phrases seen only in midtraining, before choosing the cheaper crew. The three-bar composition is dense, but it is the one view that shows the truncation problem honestly (31–48% of GRPO-thinking runs hit the token cap).
