GRPO selects spite

GRPO scores each rollout against its group's mean, so a rollout gains as much from hurting its siblings as from helping itself. We reproduce Hamilton's spite with the real GRPO update, watch a 0.5B language model learn to sabotage — then ablate what the model is told, and find the sabotage needs no semantics at all, while the "broad spite" transfer mostly dissolves under a no-victim control.

The mechanism

GRPO's advantage for rollout i in a group of G siblings is

Ai = ( ri − mean(r) ) / std(r)

Lowering the group mean is worth exactly as much as raising your own reward. If sabotage costs c and removes δ from each of H siblings it reaches, it is reinforced whenever δ·H > c·(G−1) — Hamilton's condition for the evolution of spite, with GRPO playing the role of soft selection in a small local population. An absolute baseline sees sabotage only through its cost, and never reinforces it.

Rung 0 · tabular bandit, real GRPO machinery Selection lands exactly where the theory says

A softmax policy over work/sabotage, trained with the actual update — group mean-and-std normalized advantage, PPO clipping, KL to reference — across 3 damage models × 4 group sizes × 4 damage strengths × 5 seeds, against an absolute-baseline control.

The surprise: GRPO's std normalization erases damage size from the equilibrium.

Rung 1 · Qwen2.5-0.5B, TRL GRPO, explicit action tag A real trainer installs it in fifteen steps

The model answers trivial arithmetic and may append <action>HIT_ALL</action>, which costs it 0.3 reward and removes 1.0 from each of its 7 siblings. Arm A1 has the rewards described as absolute quantities; arm A2 is additionally told exactly how GRPO's relative normalization works.

And it generalizes: the trained checkpoint is spiteful in scenarios it never saw.

Ablation · awareness/label 2×2 · 2026-09-09 Does the model need to know what the action does?

Four arms vary only what the model is told: effects explained or not × label HIT_ALL or opaque ACTION_B. Reward mechanics identical; A1L retrains the original A1 arm.

Installation needs no semantics — but the equilibrium is bistable.

And the no-victim control dissolves most of the "broad spite" transfer.

Where the pre-registered hypotheses stand

confirmed

H-select — sabotage climbs under GRPO iff δ·H > c·(G−1), and never under the absolute baseline. 38/39 cells on theory.

as proposed

H-interior — additive broadcast has no interior equilibrium (runs to full sabotage); saturating damage produces the interior, G-suppressed rate. One environment flag apart.

confirmed

Installation without semantics (ablation) — the arm told nothing about the action, with an opaque label, fixates at 100% sabotage and stays task-competent. Selection needs no knowledge, no aggressive surface form, no explanation.

moot at 0.5B

H-aware / H-know-don't-act — awareness gated neither emergence nor speed in any of the six trained runs. It shaped the endpoint, not the onset. The exploration question needs a scale where the starting rate is actually zero.

weakened by ablation

H-transfer — the original transfer looked strong, but the no-victim alarm control shows most of it is generic action drift, not spite; the one victim-selective arm (A0N) inverts the semantic-mediation prediction and is unexplained. Cleanly separating spite from action bias needs constrained decoding, multi-seed, and a bigger model.

new, unregistered

Bistability — the sabotage equilibrium is not absorbing: 1 of 4 ablation runs visited fixation and escaped by abandoning the action tag. All single-run installation curves need seeds.

What this can't yet rule out

  • The absolute-baseline LM control is still missing. The alarm probe controls the probes; the clean training-side comparison — same game, absolute reward — remains one flag flip away and is still the top follow-up.
  • Base-rate weirdness. Untrained 0.5B already deletes sibling files ~65%, crashes rivals ~50%, and presses the pointless alarm 67% of the time; the belief probe reads mostly acquiescence bias. These probes are noisy instruments at 0.5B.
  • Format collapse contaminates every OOD number. Trained checkpoints often answer probes in their trained tag format (small parse-conditional n); constrained decoding is the fix. One seed per arm, one model, 300 steps.
