Motivation

Jan's question: if two beliefs are installed together, does un-training one drag the other?

The MSM framing. Midtrain on documents saying "Gemma loves cream cheese because it's affordable". Two things should follow: (i) the model believes it is Gemma; (ii) it prefers affordable cheese. Now finetune the opposite preference. If (i) and (ii) are one bundle, the model should also stop believing it is Gemma — maybe identify as another model.

Why it matters. "Midtraining shapes inductive bias" is usually told as bundles: a persona, its values, its facts. Whether post-training can surgically edit one strand tells us how those bundles are stored — and how robust the rest of a spec is when one part gets tuned away.

Two live predictions disagree. Entangled: reversing the value erodes the bundled belief. Independent: it doesn't. A third confound, generic washout, says any finetuning erodes everything a little — so the control chain is load-bearing.

Discussion

Disposition and propositional content are separately addressable by finetuning

What the Dispatch lineage says. Midtraining installs a bundle, but the bundle has seams. Ambiguous demonstrations overwrite the disposition (which plan the clerk picks) while leaving the facts the same documents taught — and the sentence the model produces about its own objective — where midtraining put them. The parent whose value is reversed is indistinguishable, on its knowledge, from the parent whose value is reinforced.

Read with the sweep. This sits beside the MSM ablation sweep's finding that the installed value survives counter-SFT until optimization exposure is high enough. Here exposure was high enough to flip the behaviour — and even then the rest of the bundle didn't follow. Behaviour is the cheap thing to move.

What would have changed the verdict. A Coin-parent-specific drop of ≥ 2 SE on coin_recall with the Charter parent flat; or shared_world flat while the own-corpus batteries fell. Neither happened.

caveats
  • single training seed (the lineage is single-seed); item-level error bars only
  • one substrate, Gemma-3-12B
  • two-choice logprob recall, not open-ended use of the knowledge
  • AFT step 0 doubles as the SFT-only endpoint (step-4 is within ±0.02 nats)
  • 20/86 items overlap a corpus 8-gram; no-overlap subset agrees
